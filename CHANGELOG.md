# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- The default animation is now Slide: your friend slips in and slips away. Change it any time with **Water Reminder: Change Animation**.
- After you click "I've had a drink!", the character now cheers you on with one of 15 different messages, picked at random.

If you already chose an animation yourself, your choice is kept.
