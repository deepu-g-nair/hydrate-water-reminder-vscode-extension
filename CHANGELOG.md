# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- **3D-style look.** Characters are now shaded and rounded, cast a soft shadow on the ground, turn their heads to look around, and can move in depth.
- **Four new characters:** Fox, Penguin, Koala and Tiger.
- **Five new ways to arrive:** roll in like a ball, float down with a balloon, run in from far away, appear in a puff of smoke, and peek round the edge. Each has a matching way to leave.
- **A different style every time.** Random is the new default. To always use one style, pick it with **Water Reminder: Change Animation**. You can also trim the list Random picks from (Random Pool).
- **It waits for you.** The character now stays until you click "I've had a drink!" or Dismiss, and gives a small wave, hop or twirl every so often. Turn on **Auto Close** in Settings for the old behaviour.
- **Sound.** A soft chime when the character arrives and when it leaves, in five styles (Modern, Elegant, Warm, Water, Minimal). Run **Water Reminder: Change Sound** to hear them and pick one, or to turn sound off.
- **System notification.** If a reminder is due while you are working in another application, you also get a notification from your operating system. Turn it off with the **System Notification** setting.
- **Several VS Code windows now share one reminder.** It appears in the window you are using; answering it once restarts the countdown everywhere. Pause, Resume and Reset apply to all windows.
- Works in Restricted Mode and in remote windows (SSH, WSL, containers).

**Your settings start fresh.** Because so many defaults changed, the first time this version runs it resets your saved Water Reminder settings to the new defaults and shows a short notice. This happens once; anything you change afterwards is kept. Settings saved for a single workspace are not touched.

## 0.2.0

- The default animation is now Slide: your friend slips in and slips away. Change it any time with **Water Reminder: Change Animation**.
- After you click "I've had a drink!", the character now cheers you on with one of 15 different messages, picked at random.

If you already chose an animation yourself, your choice is kept.
